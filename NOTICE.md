## The grant

The remix grant is spoken by the artist, in the recording, at 3:02–3:22
(`t=182`). It is quoted here under that grant:

> "You can use the music how you want. That's the truth right from the start.
> Sample this and remix that. Cut it up and bring it back. Credit would be nice
> if you do. If it blows, throw something back."

[Listen at 3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ms0UbxUV9X8&t=182).

The grant is **permissive but credit-seeking**, not a formal instrument.
`le.go` records it as `formalInstrument: null` and honours all three limbs:
credit, fragment-only quotation, and handing the model back to the community.

Where the grant is silent (resale, commercial use, attribution format), `le.go`
records the silence as silence. It does not assert terms the artist did not
speak. A 24 h revocation takedown / 72 h tombstone path is modelled in the data
and honoured in the UI.

## Privacy invariant (zero exceptions)

The source work's link hub includes a mutual-aid fundraiser whose subject is a
named third party. Per the `42i` filtration rule, `le.go` models this **only**
as an isotope:

- the **real-axis claim** — *"the artist's public profile links a crowdfunding
  campaign for a third party"* — is citable and rendered;
- the **latent predicate** (name, condition, diagnosis, figures) is held
  unasserted at `0 + 42i`, never rendered, and never transcribed.

**No third-party name, medical condition, diagnosis, or dollar figure appears
anywhere in this repository, its git history, its build output, or its commit
messages.** The publish guard fails closed if one is introduced.
