Three people, sixty links in a group chat, one paper and one video essay due Friday. Every link has a different shape, a different share token, and a different amount of ad.

## The pile collapses

Paste the pile into the [`/projector`](/projector) reverse mode. The six URL shapes collapse to one work key:

```text

```

The `si=` token is scrubbed; the `RD…` radio context is recorded as a boolean; the `&t=182` is noted as a pointer *inside* the work.

## One work, many locators

When three of you each copy a lyric line, the ledger in [`/philosophy`](/philosophy) deduplicates them into **one bibliography work entry + line locators**:

- Export **BibTeX / RIS / CSL-JSON** for the paper.
- Export a **chapter clip-sheet** (`M:SS`) for the video discussion.

You never list *Open Arms* forty times; you list it once, with forty locators.

## The rule that keeps you honest

Record your **consumption surface** — `youtube`, `youtube_music`, or `/bare/`. The citation is the event; the medium of consumption is the projection. If you read the ad-free mirror, say so; the event doesn't move, but the footnote shouldn't lie about the window you used.
