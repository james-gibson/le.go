## The grant

At **3:02–3:22** (`t=182`), the artist grants the remix himself, in the song:

> "You can use the music how you want. That's the truth right from the start. Sample this and remix that. Cut it up and bring it back. Credit would be nice if you do. If it blows, throw something back."

The grant is **permissive but credit-seeking**, not a formal licence: *use how you want, credit if you can, reciprocate if it lands*. `le.go` honours all three limbs — it credits, it quotes fragments, and it hands the model back to the community — and it records the grant itself as data rather than leaving it in prose.

Where the grant is silent (resale, commercial use, attribution *format*), `le.go` records **silence as silence**. It does not assert a licence the artist did not speak.

## The disclaimer

The song also opens with its own disclaimer, treated here as a scoping constraint rather than decoration:

> "This ain't a diss to musicians, performers, or anybody protecting what they built. I respect that… I'm just speaking for me."
