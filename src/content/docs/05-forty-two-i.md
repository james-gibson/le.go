Here is where the Douglas Adams joke stops being a joke and becomes a type system.

## The premise, in four snaps

| Piece | The move | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `null` | leave the hole empty | absence is **no plane** — never a value you can put in the graph |
| `not null` | the hole is a piece | *"null exists"* is itself a fact; unknown ≠ absent |
| `0 > 1` | a silent zero outranks a spoken one | a zeroed field is a *claim* that disconnects the graph (`ErrDisconnected`) |
| `42 = 0` | every universe agrees where zero sits | `42` is the shared origin, so you can rotate into `0 + 42i` without drifting off the plane |

## `42` is a coordinate, not a credential

`42` is **the shared origin coordinate** — the point every universe agrees on as zero. It is not the punchline to an unasked question; it is where you measure *from*, so nobody drifts off the plane without noticing.

The plane accepts exactly two anchor tags — `42` and `42i` — and rejects everything else:

| Rotation | Arithmetic | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R⁰ | `42 + 0i` | proven consensus — asserted, carries the corpus charge |
| R¹ | `i·42 = 0 + 42i` | register moves to **working hypothesis** — not asserted, retrievable |
| R² | `i²·42 = −42` | sign flips — this is `relationshipType: contradicts` |
| R³ | `−42i` | hypothesis in the opposing sector |
| R⁴ | `+42` | identity (i⁴ = 1) |

Two consequences fall out of the model, not out of taste:

1. **Rotation count is metadata, never a plane label.** There is no `42i²` anchor; the anchor stays `42` or `42i`, and `rotation.count ∈ {0,1,2,3}`.
2. **R² is not a no-op.** Two turns land on the corpus's existing sign-flip semantics — a doubly-rotated statement is a *contradiction* and must carry a `contradicts` edge.

## The quarter-turn does ethical work

An edge is admissible only when the phases cancel:

```text
q_a + q_b = 0
```

A record holding only `q_a = +1` is a **half-edge that cannot enter the mesh** — not because a policy forbids it, but because it is disconnected by construction.

So the artist's mutual-aid fundraiser edge is modelled as an **isotope**:

| Isotope | Mass number | Components | Citable? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Z-0 | 0 latent | runs a community-forward profile | yes, asserted |
| Z-1 | 1 latent | *plus*: hub links a crowdfunding campaign for a third party | yes — a claim about **what he links** |
| Z-2 | 2 latent | *plus*: the subject's condition and figures | **never asserted** — retained only |

The latent component adds **retrieval mass without adding charge**. The corpus holds the real-axis claim; the sensitive predicate sits at `0 + 42i`, `assertable: false`, addressable only through the reader's own click-through. No name, no condition, no figures enter the record — and the **zeroed field** that *looks* safe is the one the graph rejects.

## Casting shadows back

Because the complex tuple is stored, projecting to Chicago, MLA, APA, BibTeX, or `/bare/` is matrix multiplication with punctuation templates. Nothing is lost until you ask for a flat shadow — and you can cast a fresh shadow whenever an editor asks.
