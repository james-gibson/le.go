`le.go` is a `le.gume` seed: *a body of knowledge with a stated audience, a stated assumption, and one entrance.* It lifts the **Silverskin** isotope from `le.gume-energy` and plants a new specimen inside it.

## What "Silverskin" means

The silverskin is the thin membrane around a legume seed — the part you peel to reveal the seed proper. In the `le.gume` family:

| Term | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `le.gume` | the pod — a body of knowledge that splits cleanly along two seams |
| `Silverskin` | the membrane — the content model, derived navigation, guard, and deploy |
| the seed | the specific body of knowledge planted inside |

## The two seams

A legume splits along two seams:

1. **local time + UTC** — the work's own clock and the shared tick.
2. **real axis + imaginary axis** — `42 + 0i` proven consensus and `0 + 42i` working hypothesis.

The seed holds countable, shelf-stable peas in order (`l001`, `l002`, …) — which is exactly what the line-addressed `/philosophy` page is.
